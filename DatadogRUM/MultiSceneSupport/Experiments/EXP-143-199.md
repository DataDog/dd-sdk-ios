## EXP-143 — External semantic-router mutations and restoration

Status: planned; no run or backend result claimed.

### Question

Does the experimental iOS 27 semantic `RUMNavigationStack` preserve committed
occurrence semantics when the application's router, rather than a destination
link, replaces or rejects the path, and when the stack restores directly into
repeated equal values?

### Required cases

- programmatic same-type and different-type replacement;
- a router that rejects a proposed path write;
- a router that canonicalizes a proposed write;
- restoration to one route and directly to two equal routes;
- lifecycle work attributed only after the accepted destination occurrence is
  current; and
- no change to native `NavigationLink(value: Route)` matching.

### Acceptance

The application binding remains authoritative. Rejected proposals create no RUM
transition; canonicalized proposals create only the accepted path. Every
materialized repeated path position has a distinct RUM UUID, and a revealed
destination starts fresh before its first lifecycle callback work. Mapper and
backend view/action/Resource inventories must agree, with no automatic duplicate,
error, or crash.
