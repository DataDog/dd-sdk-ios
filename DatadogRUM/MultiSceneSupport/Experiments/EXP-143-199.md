## EXP-143 — External semantic-router mutations and restoration

Status: PASS after FAIL, rejected candidates, and tooling-invalid retries ·
backend. Repeated-path restoration, external replacement, rejected writes, and
canonicalized writes are accepted with pre-view lifecycle attribution.

### Question

Does the experimental iOS 27 semantic `RUMNavigationStack` preserve committed
occurrence semantics when the application's router, rather than a destination
link, replaces or rejects the path, and when the stack restores directly into
repeated equal values?

### Required cases

- programmatic same-type and different-type replacement;
- a router that rejects a proposed path write;
- a router that canonicalizes a proposed write;
- restoration to one route and directly to two equal routes;
- lifecycle work attributed only after the accepted destination occurrence is
  current; and
- no change to native `NavigationLink(value: Route)` matching.

### Acceptance

The application binding remains authoritative. Rejected proposals create no RUM
transition; canonicalized proposals create only the accepted path. Every
materialized repeated path position has a distinct RUM UUID, and a revealed
destination starts fresh before its first lifecycle callback work. Mapper and
backend view/action/Resource inventories must agree, with no automatic duplicate,
error, or crash.

### Conclusion and next boundary

iOS 27 restoration is lazy: the root and current top exist initially, while a
lower equal destination does not. A non-current boundary must lend concrete scene
proof without starting a RUM view, while the source owns the last proven visible
destination until its accepted boundary materializes. Reliable reader mounts may
transfer that ownership or recover it fresh after disconnect; delayed trait,
refresh, detach, and disappear callbacks remain fenced.

External router writes expose a second platform behavior: SwiftUI may reuse the
speculative proposal's boundary for a different value returned by a canonicalizing
binding. The accepted getter value, never the proposal, is authoritative. On iOS
27 the inherited scene trait can advance that already-proven same-scene dormant
boundary during body evaluation, before customer lifecycle work. It is not scene
proof and cannot migrate or resurrect state. Replacement, rejection,
canonicalization, restoration, mapper attribution, backend intake, full tests,
lint, and the Release probe build now pass. `EXP-143` is closed.

## EXP-144 — Semantic presentation replacement

Status: PASS after SDK FAIL and harness-invalid retry · backend. Direct Sheet →
full-screen Cover → Sheet replacement is atomic, never exposes the underlying
Home between presentations, and reveals one fresh Home after final dismissal.

### Question

Does the experimental iOS 27 semantic `RUMNavigationStack` treat a direct
non-`nil` presentation-binding replacement as one committed destination change,
without briefly revealing the underlying stack destination or pinning all later
presentations to the first occurrence?

The accepted path is:

```text
Home H1 → Sheet S1 → Cover F1 → Sheet S2 → Home H2
```

Every presentation occurrence must have a distinct RUM view ID, automatic
tracking must create no duplicate, and the final H2 must start before immediate
and settled post-dismiss customer work.

- Scenario: `swiftui.semantic-api.presentation-replacement`.
- Environment: clean iPad Pro 13-inch (M5) simulator,
  `B4E4F039-CA5D-4D04-A904-1D71099BE651`, iPadOS 27.0, Xcode 27.0.
- Automatic SwiftUI tracking remained enabled around the actual experimental
  semantic-navigation SPI.
- Every conclusive attempt terminated and uninstalled
  `com.datadoghq.rum-native-multi-scene-probe`, then proved the app container was
  absent before installation.
- SDK implementation commit: signed `698b1584d` (`Handle semantic presentation
  replacement`). It holds the last mounted outgoing presentation until the

## EXP-146 — Container-independent semantic navigation host

### Question

SwiftUI container through an outer host and stable type-erased transition source,
while leaving native `NavigationStack`, `.sheet`, and `.fullScreenCover` code
unchanged and preserving exact occurrence, lifecycle, authority, and downstream
attribution semantics?

This experiment asks whether the engine and adapter boundary are correct. Its
explicit `RUMNavigationTransitions` publisher is a deterministic harness adapter,
not evidence that normal applications should publish every transition or maintain

### Preconditions and implementation revision

The implementation is split across signed commits:

  experimental arbitrary-content `RUMNavigationHost`, a stable

consumes the already published iOS 27 scene trait during body evaluation, before
descendant lifecycle callbacks. Customers do not provide an internal RUM UUID or
native scene identifier.

The probe's low-level `willNavigate` and `commit` calls are adapter controls. They
prove prepared/committed/cancelled transition semantics, but are not the proposed
normal customer API. A viable integration still needs automatic metadata by
default, sparse optional overrides, and approximately one integration per
container or existing router rather than edits proportional to destinations or
navigation methods. `EXP-147` subsequently measures and accepts that migration
