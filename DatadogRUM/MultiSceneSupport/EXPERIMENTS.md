# RUM multi-scene experiment index

This is a lookup index, not a restart cursor. Related gates identify scope and do
not imply closure; [release-gates.json](release-gates.json) owns current status.
[.continue-here.md](../../.continue-here.md) exclusively owns current work.
Each outcome is bounded to that experiment's frozen revision and environment.

PASS does not erase earlier failed/invalid/inconclusive attempts. Mixed outcomes
preserve their limits. PLANNED means defined; PREPARED means driver/oracle ready
but acceptance pending. “SDK PASS; native PLANNED” does not close a runtime gate.
Detailed records and durable results own exact counts, identities and attempts.

Archived links point to exact detailed-log lines (some early experiments share a
chronological section). These are lookup locations, not reusable run/session IDs.
Active records use stable section anchors. Do not edit or load the frozen archive
wholesale. [Lookup rules](Archive/README.md) and [rejected lessons](REJECTED_APPROACHES.md)
remain available for a specific question.

Post-publication follow-ups retain their existing release-gate ownership: [Resource review](Results/S1-resource-completion-review.json), [hitch assertions](Results/S1-hitch-review.json), and [current CI](Results/S1-ci-followup.json). The [E01 test witness](Results/S1-resume-witness-review.json) and [Duo environment readiness](Results/S2-Duo-environment-readiness.json) stay within those existing release gates. The completed local timeseries checks are retained in the CI record; upstream PR3196 owns that fix. Unattributed CI-flake repairs are out of scope under the latest user instruction. The [M10 source audit](Results/S3-M10-source-audit.json) records lint and API comparison under F03 without allocating another experiment.
